# 7. An Overview of Providers

This chapter introduces the primary market leaders in cloud computing, their key offerings, and the core factors organizations must consider when selecting a cloud provider.

## 1. Cloud Market Landscape & Key Players

- **Market Focus:** Market share analyses typically concentrate on **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**, **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**, and **Hosted Private Cloud Services**.
    
- **Market Dominance:** As of late 2023, the cloud market is dominated by **three major providers** (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform).
    
- **Dynamic Environment:** The cloud computing landscape evolves rapidly—products are updated, renamed, or deprecated, and customer relationships change over time.
    

## 2. Business Impact of Cloud Adoption

By leveraging IaaS and PaaS offerings, organizations can achieve:

- **Greater Business Agility & Innovation:** Accelerate product delivery and adapt quickly to market demands.
    
- **Operational Efficiency:** Optimize costs and reduce time spent managing physical hardware, allowing teams to focus on core business goals.
    

## 3. Core Criteria for Choosing a Cloud Provider

Selecting the right cloud provider depends on an organization's specific technical and business needs. Key considerations include:

- **Infrastructure & Data Center Costs:** Comparing current on-premise hardware and facility maintenance costs against cloud operational expenses.
    
- **Migration & Application Rework:** Factoring in the expense of migrating, refactoring, or completely rebuilding existing applications for cloud compatibility.
    
- **Workforce & Skill Requirements:** Evaluating the costs of hiring or training internal cloud specialists.
    
- **Risk & Benefit Analysis:** Weighing the security, reliability, and agility gains against potential migration risks.
    

### Quick Review Questions

1. **Which specific cloud service tiers are primarily measured when determining market share among providers?**
    
    - _Answer:_ Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Hosted Private Cloud Services.
        
2. **Why might a company need to refactor or rebuild applications during cloud adoption?**
    
    - _Answer:_ Legacy on-premise applications may need to be updated to take full advantage of cloud-native architectures and PaaS environments.
        
3. **What is the primary guiding principle when selecting a cloud provider?**
    
    - _Answer:_ The best cloud provider is the one that aligns best with an organization's specific technical requirements, workload needs, and budget constraints.
